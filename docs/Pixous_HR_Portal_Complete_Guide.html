1. Project Overview

In one sentence: Pixous HR Portal is a complete employee management system where a company can manage attendance, leave, payroll, chat, tasks, assets, and everything related to running its workforce — all from one website and mobile app.

What is the purpose of this project?

Every company has employees. Those employees come to work, take leaves, get paid, submit complaints, use company assets, and communicate with each other. Today, most companies use WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets, and paper forms to manage all of this.

Pixous HR Portal replaces all of that with one single application. It is a website and a mobile app that lets every person in the company — from the newest employee to the CEO — do their daily work digitally.

What makes it special?

  • Real-time: When someone sends a message, it appears instantly. When a manager approves leave, the employee sees it immediately. No refresh button needed.
  • Multi-tenant: The same system can be used by many different companies. Each company sees only its own data.
  • Role-based: An employee sees only what an employee should see. An HR manager sees more. The System Admin sees everything. Nobody sees what they should not see.
  • Mobile + Web: Works on desktop browsers and as an Android mobile app with the same features.
  • Face Verification: Attendance punching uses your face to confirm it is really you.
  • GPS Tracking: When you punch in from outside the office, your location is recorded.
  • AI Insights: A Python-based AI microservice analyzes attendance patterns and gives you smart insights.

2. User Roles

The system has different types of users. Each role has different permissions — what they can see, what they can do, and what pages appear in their sidebar.

Employee — IT Employee / Field Employee

An ordinary team member. They can punch in/out, apply for leave, view their payslips, chat with colleagues, see tasks assigned to them, submit support tickets, and view their own profile. They cannot approve leave, manage payroll, or see other employees' attendance.

Team Leader

A team leader has everything an Employee has, plus: they can approve leave requests from their team members (up to 3 days), see team attendance, assign tasks to team members, and view team reports.

HR / HR Head

HR has everything a Team Leader has, plus: they can approve leave requests of any duration, manage payroll and generate payslips, manage leave policies, manage employee onboarding, see the full employee directory, view all attendance, and manage complaints.

CTO (System Admin)

The highest role. Sees everything. Can manage all modules, all employees, all settings. The CTO account is also the System Admin — full access to every page and every button in the system.

System Admin / Super Admin

The technical administrator. Can manage modules (turn features on/off), manage users, configure branding, view the audit log, and reset company data. This role sits in the "Tech Admin" panel.

3. Login — Getting Started

When you open the Pixous HR Portal, the first screen you see is the Login page. This is the front door to the entire system.

What you see on the screen

  • A beautiful background video playing behind a glass-style login card
  • The Pixous Technologies logo in the top-right corner of the card
  • Two input fields: Username and Password
  • A Show/Hide Password eye icon button
  • A large Sign In button

Step-by-step: How login works

  1. Type your username — this can be your username or your full name
  2. Type your password — the characters are hidden as dots by default
  3. Click the eye icon (👁) to show/hide the password — useful for checking you typed it correctly
  4. Click "Sign In" — the button shows a spinner while it checks your credentials
  5. If correct: You are taken to the Dashboard. A green "Welcome back" message appears briefly.
  6. If wrong: A red error message appears: "Login failed — check your username and password"

What happens behind the scenes

When you click Sign In, the system sends your username and password to the server. The server checks if this combination exists in the database. If yes, it creates a secure access token (like a digital key) and sends it back. This token proves you are logged in for the rest of your session. If you close the browser and come back, the system remembers you and takes you directly to the Dashboard without asking for login again.

5. Attendance — Punch In & Out

The Attendance page lets you record when you start and end your work day. It uses your face and GPS location to verify it is really you at the right place.

What you see on the screen

  • Today's Card — Shows your punch-in time, punch-out time, and current status
  • Mode Selector — Three options: Office Work from Home Site / Field
  • Punch In / Punch Out Button — The main action button
  • Face Status Panel — Shows if your face is registered, and allows face-based punching
  • Monthly Summary — A row of 5 stat boxes: Present, WFH, Late, Absent, Overtime
  • Attendance Insights — AI-generated observations about your team's attendance patterns
  • History Table — Your past attendance records with pagination

Step-by-step: How to punch in

  1. Go to the Attendance page from the sidebar
  2. Choose your work mode:
    • Office — You are working from the office. GPS will be captured.
    • Work from Home — You are at home. No GPS needed.
    • Site / Field — You are at a client site or field location. GPS will be captured and checked against the office geofence.
  3. Click Punch in with face — your camera opens
  4. Look at the camera — the system matches your face with the registered face photo
  5. If the face matches: your punch is recorded with GPS location and time. A success message appears.
  6. If no face is registered: a message tells you to ask HR to register your face first (takes 4 quick photos).

Step-by-step: How to punch out

  1. When your work day is over, go back to the Attendance page
  2. Click Punch out with face
  3. The system records your punch-out time and calculates your total hours worked
  4. A green message says: "Day complete — see you tomorrow."

Attendance History Table

Below the punch card, there is a table showing your attendance history. Each row shows:

  • Date — The day (e.g., "Mon, 18 Aug")
  • Employee ID — Your employee code
  • In — What time you punched in
  • Out — What time you punched out
  • Mode — Office / WFH / SITE
  • Status — PRESENT, ABSENT, or LATE badge
  • View button — Click to see all details for that day (GPS coordinates, hours worked, overtime, etc.)

6. Leave Management — Applying for Leave

The Leave page lets you check how many leave days you have, apply for new leave, and track your requests.

What you see on the screen

  • Leave Balance Cards — A row of cards showing each leave type (Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Earned Leave, etc.) with how many days are available out of total allocated
  • "Apply for leave" button — The green button to create a new leave request
  • "Export Excel" button — Downloads your leave history as an Excel file
  • From/To month selectors — Filter which months to show in the history
  • My Requests Table — A table of all your leave requests with status, dates, and actions

Step-by-step: How to apply for leave

  1. Click + Apply for leave button — a form opens
  2. Choose leave type — Select from the dropdown (Casual Leave, Sick Leave, etc.)
  3. Pick start date — Click the "From" date picker and choose the first day of your leave
  4. Pick end date — Click the "To" date picker and choose the last day of your leave
  5. Choose approver — The system automatically shows the right person:
    • If your leave is 3 days or less → it goes to your Team Leader
    • If your leave is more than 3 days → it goes to HR
  6. Write a reason — Explain why you need leave (this is required)
  7. Click Submit request
  8. A green message says: "Leave request submitted"
  9. Your request appears in the table with status "PENDING"

Leave Request Table — Action Buttons

7. Short Leave (Permissions)

A "Permission" is a short leave — maybe one or two hours for a doctor's appointment, or half a day for personal work. It is different from regular leave because it does not deduct from your leave balance.

Step-by-step: How to apply for a permission

  1. Go to Leave Management → Permission in the sidebar
  2. Click Apply for permission
  3. Choose whether you need one hour or two hours
  4. Pick the date and time you need to leave
  5. Write a reason
  6. Choose who should approve it
  7. Click Submit

What the Manager sees

Team Leaders and HR can see all pending permission requests. Each request shows the employee's name, date, duration, and reason. The manager clicks Approve or Reject to decide.

9. Tasks — Work Assignment

The Tasks module lets managers assign work to employees and track its progress. Employees can update their task status and add comments.

What you see on the screen

  • Stat Tiles — Counts of tasks by status: Not Started, In Progress, Completed
  • Monthly Summary Card — A chart showing task completion trends
  • Task Groups — Tasks grouped by assigner (who assigned them to you)
  • Search bar — Search tasks by title
  • Filter buttons — Filter by "My Tasks" or "Assigned by Me"

For Managers: How to assign a task

  1. Click + Assign Task — a form opens
  2. Choose Team mode or Individual mode:
    • Team mode: Select a team (designation) → check/uncheck team members → task goes to all selected members
    • Individual mode: Choose Digital or Infra division → pick one employee
  3. Type the task title (required)
  4. Type a description (optional)
  5. Pick a due date (optional)
  6. Click Assign Task
  7. Each assigned employee gets a notification

For Employees: How to update a task

  1. Click on a task to expand it
  2. You will see the task title, description, assigner, and due date
  3. Click the Status dropdown to change:
    • Not started → In progress → Completed
  4. You can also add comments by typing in the comment box and clicking Send
  5. You can attach files by clicking the paperclip icon 📎

    13. Payroll & Payslips

    The Payroll module handles salary processing and payslip generation. HR generates payslips; employees view and download theirs.

    For HR: Payroll Requests

    1. Go to Payroll → Payroll in the sidebar
    2. You see a list of employees who have requested their payslip
    3. Click Generate next to an employee's name
    4. A detailed form opens with:
      • Earnings section: Basic salary, HRA, Allowances, Overtime pay
      • Deductions section: PF, ESI, PT, TDS, Other deductions
      • Working days and Pay date
      • Company logo upload — optional logo that appears on the payslip
      • Net pay — automatically calculated (Earnings minus Deductions)
    5. Fill in the amounts and click Generate & Send
    6. The payslip is created and sent to the employee as a notification

    For Employees: Payslips

    1. Go to Payroll → Payslips in the sidebar
    2. You see a list of all your past payslips
    3. Click any payslip to view the full breakdown (earnings, deductions, net pay)
    4. Click Download PDF to save a copy

    28. Mobile App Overview

    The mobile app is an Android application that provides the same features as the website, optimized for phone screens.

    How is the mobile app different from the website?

    • Bottom navigation instead of a sidebar — 5 main tabs: Home, Attendance, Chat, More, Profile
    • Touch-friendly — bigger buttons, swipe gestures, pull-to-refresh
    • Camera access — directly opens the phone camera for face verification
    • GPS access — uses the phone's built-in GPS for location during punch-in
    • Push notifications — alerts appear even when the app is closed
    • Offline handling — graceful messages when there is no internet connection
